## New

- **The Office.** Each project can now show its agents in a startup office instead of the kitchen: click the gear in the scene's corner and choose **Office**. Agents read tickets at the ticket board, plan at the whiteboards, write code at the desks (their laptop screens light up), run commands at the server rack, test at the QA bench, and present finished work at the demo table, where clients in the lobby receive it. An agent that needs you rings the manager's desk bell, and the manager comes over. The choice is saved per project.
- The gear also holds the scene's **Sounds** switch.

## Kitchen

- An agent that needs you now waits in front of the gong, with the head chef beside it.
- The break room is quiet, keeps the six most recently active agents, and the vending machine has replaced the coffee bar.
- The serving window is an open counter.
- The camera looks at the kitchen from a lower angle, so you can see the chefs' faces.
