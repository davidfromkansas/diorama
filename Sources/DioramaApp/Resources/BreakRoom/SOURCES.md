# Break room furniture sources

## EamesLoungeLow.obj / .mtl

Herman Miller Eames Lounge Chair–Classic. Copyright remains with Herman Miller / MillerKnoll;
this is not a generically licensed asset. The project owner confirmed permission to convert and
bundle it in Diorama on 2026-09-29 — do not treat it as freely redistributable.

- https://www.hermanmiller.com/resources/3d-models-and-planning-tools/product-models/individual/eames-lounge-chair-classic/

Converted from the official DWG with LibreDWG and decimated in Blender (2026-10-02). The DWG's
disconnected ottoman is omitted; chair proportions are preserved. Black leather and walnut shell
colors are Diorama presentation choices. Moved here from the retired `OfficeFurniture` folder; the
chef sitting clips are tuned to this chair's seat height (`assets/chef/README.md`).

The arcade, espresso and vending GLBs are built by `assets/breakroom/build.sh`.
